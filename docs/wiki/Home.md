# Welcome to the BlockGame Wiki

**BlockGame** is a procedural voxel sandbox survival game built in **Rust** and **wgpu** for **Linux** and **Windows**, supporting both singleplayer worlds and dedicated multiplayer servers.

## Wiki Pages

- **[Getting Started](Getting-Started)** — Downloading releases, building from source on Linux/Windows, and launching the client.
- **[Controls and UI](Controls-and-UI)** — Movement, interaction, inventory, portable backpacks (`B`), skills menu (`K`), achievements (`J`), armor slots, and JEI recipe browser shortcuts.
- **[Character Creator](Character-Creator)** — Customizing your stylized low-poly character, Body Frames, hairstyles, clothing, RGB colors, presets, and multiplayer sync.
- **[Gameplay and Survival](Gameplay-and-Survival)** — Crafting, smelting, MMO skills (1–1000), fishing minigame, Creative Mode, tools, armor, hunger/saturation, beds, and dynamic music.
- **[Achievements](Achievements)** — Profile-wide Achievements (42 total across 8 gameplay pillars plus spoiler-protected Hidden Achievements), Career Stats, Achievement Eligibility, and HUD unlock toasts.
- **[Farming and Cooking](Farming-and-Cooking)** — Crops & Stem Crops, passive soil Fertilizers (Compost & Bone Fertilizer), Cooking Pot meals, two-tone Hunger/Saturation HUD, status effects, and Herbal Tea.
- **[Power and Storage](Power-and-Storage)** — EU power generation (Coal, Solar, Wind), tiered Battery Boxes & Cables, Electric Machines, Iron/Gold/Diamond Chests, Powered Storage Terminals, and unique Portable Backpacks.
- **[Mobs and Breeding](Mobs-and-Breeding)** — Passive animals, species-specific Breeding Feeds & herding, Cow milking, Chicken eggs, hostile monsters, spawning rules, and combat.
- **[World and Biomes](World-and-Biomes)** — The 20 procedural biomes, macro-biome sizing, lighting, caves, water physics, block orientation persistence, and world saves.
- **[Multiplayer and Server Guide](Multiplayer-and-Server-Guide)** — Hosting `blockgame_server`, CLI flags, and connecting clients.
- **[Commands and Permissions](Commands-and-Permissions)** — Console/chat commands (`/gamemode`, `/skills`, waypoints, admin), ranks, groups, and permission nodes.
- **[Building and Releasing](Building-and-Releasing)** — Automated GitHub Actions builds and manual release packaging.
