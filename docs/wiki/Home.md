# Welcome to the BlockGame Wiki

**BlockGame** is a procedural voxel sandbox survival game built in **Rust** and **wgpu** for **Linux** and **Windows**, supporting both singleplayer worlds and dedicated multiplayer servers.

## Wiki Pages

- **[Getting Started](Getting-Started)** — Downloading releases, building from source on Linux/Windows, and launching the client.
- **[Controls and UI](Controls-and-UI)** — Movement, interaction, inventory, armor slots, and JEI recipe browser shortcuts.
- **[Gameplay and Survival](Gameplay-and-Survival)** — Crafting, smelting, tools, armor, food, beds, and the day/night cycle.
- **[Mobs and Breeding](Mobs-and-Breeding)** — Passive animals, wheat breeding, hostile monsters, spawning rules, and combat.
- **[World and Biomes](World-and-Biomes)** — The 16 procedural biomes, lighting, caves, water physics, and world saves.
- **[Multiplayer and Server Guide](Multiplayer-and-Server-Guide)** — Hosting `blockgame_server`, CLI flags, and connecting clients.
- **[Commands and Permissions](Commands-and-Permissions)** — Console/chat commands, ranks, groups, and permission nodes.
- **[Building and Releasing](Building-and-Releasing)** — Automated GitHub Actions builds and manual release packaging.
